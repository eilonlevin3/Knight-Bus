# ADR-0003: OpenStreetMap raster tiles cached on view, with offline vector maps later

**Status:** Accepted for MVP · 2026-10-09

## Context

The requirement says the app "downloads the local metropolitan area maps". There are two
kinds of map data:

1. **Transit data**: stops, routes, the shapes of their paths. This is ours, built by the pipeline.
2. **Base map**: streets and buildings, drawn as tiles.

Pre-downloading a whole city of OpenStreetMap raster tiles is against the
[OSM tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) (no bulk
downloading). Downloading a city at zoom 12–17 would also take hundreds of MB.

## Decision

- **Transit data is downloaded per city, ahead of time** (REQ-DATA-1): 0.6 MB (Be'er Sheva) to 3.1 MB (Tel Aviv) as of Oct 2026.
- **Base map tiles** come from OSM through [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com) and are **cached
  as they are viewed** by [sw.js](../../sw.js) (REQ-DATA-3), up to 3,000 tiles. Your
  usual commute area therefore works on a weak connection.

## Later (REQ-DATA-4)

Switch to **vector tiles**: build one [PMTiles](https://docs.protomaps.com/) file per city
from an OSM extract in the same nightly pipeline, about 20–60 MB per city, and render it with
MapLibre GL. That gives a fully offline city map with our own styling, and no OSM tile
policy issue.
